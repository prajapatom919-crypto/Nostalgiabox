Copyright & Attribution Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 · Nostalgia Box

Nostalgia Box operates as an educational and cultural archival tribute to the timeless music, television themes, and cartoon anthems that defined past generations. We maintain the deepest respect for artists, composers, songwriters, record labels, animation studios, and copyright holders.

1. Content Delivery & Third-Party Streaming

Nostalgia Box does not host, upload, re-encode, or store any proprietary audio or video files on our servers. All media streams are delivered strictly via the official, publicly available YouTube IFrame Player API.

When media is played on Nostalgia Box:

  • The content streams directly from YouTube servers.
  • Views, monetization, and advertising rights remain entirely with the original copyright holders and uploaders on YouTube.
  • Direct attribution links are provided on the player card (via the YouTube icon) to allow visitors to view and support the original upload directly on YouTube.

2. Fair Use and Archival Intent

The inclusion of titles, release years, thematic classifications, and accompanying commentary is intended for non-commercial commentary, archival preservation, criticism, and educational celebration under applicable fair use principles.

3. Copyright Concerns & Removal Procedure (DMCA / Notice & Takedown)

If you are a copyright owner, artist, or authorized representative and believe that any playlist item, thumbnail, or reference featured on Nostalgia Box infringes upon your rights or should be excluded from our curation, we will gladly and promptly address your request.

To submit a copyright notice or removal request, please email our editorial team with the following details:

1. Identification: Title and artist of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
2. URL / Link: The specific YouTube URL or playlist entry displayed on Nostalgia Box.
3. Authority: Proof of ownership or authorized agency representation.
4. Contact Info: Your name, email address, and official organization (if applicable).

Please send your request to our designated contact on our Contact Page. We review and process all legitimate removal notices within 24 to 48 hours.
