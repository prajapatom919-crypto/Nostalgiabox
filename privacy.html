Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 · Nostalgia Box

At Nostalgia Box (accessible via our official domain), the privacy of our visitors is one of our utmost priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by Nostalgia Box and how we use it to maintain a safe, functional, and respectful archival experience.

1. Basic Information Collection & Log Files

Nostalgia Box follows standard web hosting practices by utilizing server log files. The information gathered inside these log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamps, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. This data is not linked to any personally identifiable information (PII). Its sole purpose is to analyze trends, administer the website, track user movements on the site, and gather broad demographic data for technical diagnostics.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like most modern websites, Nostalgia Box uses "cookies" to store information about visitors' preferences and the specific pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. This information is used to optimize the user experience by customizing web page content based on visitors' browser types and preferences (such as remembering volume levels or interface state).

3. Google Services and Advertising (Google AdSense & Analytics)

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. Google uses cookies, commonly known as DART or DoubleClick cookies, to serve advertisements to visitors of our site based upon their visit to Nostalgia Box and other websites across the internet.

Visitors may choose to opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy or by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative Opt-Out Page.

We also use Google Analytics (gtag.js) to understand how visitors engage with our archival content. Google Analytics utilizes first-party cookies to report on user interactions without identifying individual visitors personally.

4. Third-Party Embedded Players (YouTube IFrame API)

To provide audio playback for nostalgic themes and songs, Nostalgia Box interfaces with the YouTube IFrame Player API. When you play a track, data exchanges occur directly between your browser and YouTube/Google servers according to Google's Privacy Policy. Nostalgia Box does not capture, store, or sell any personal YouTube profile data or private credentials.

5. Data Retention & User Rights

We do not maintain user account registrations, passwords, or credit card processing on Nostalgia Box. Any voluntary communication sent to our editorial email or social channels is kept strictly confidential and used solely to address user inquiries, copyright notices, or content recommendations.

6. Consent and Inquiries

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out via our Contact Page.
